# Lantern Ring Mono PCB

Production boards use 0.8 mm FR-4, 1 oz copper, and lead-free HASL. Each
assembled variant fits the same LED MPN at D1-D6; TP1 and TP2 are copper
contacts and are not fitted components.

| Variant | LED MPN | Solder mask | Silkscreen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| White / Life | `IN-S126ASUW` | White | Black |
| UV / Death | `SM1206UV-400-IL` | Black | White |
| Red / Rage | `APTL3216SURCK` | Red | White |
| Orange / Avarice | `APTL3216SECK-01` | Yellow | Black |
| Yellow / Fear | `APTL3216SYCK` | Yellow | Black |
| Green / Will | `APTD3216MGC` | Green | White |
| Blue / Hope | `APT3216QBC/D` | Blue | White |
| Indigo / Compassion | `APTR3216-VFX` | Purple | White |
| Pink / Love | `APT3216PBC/RV` | Purple | White |
| Invisible / Infrared | `AP3216F3C` | Black | White |

Orange, indigo, pink, and infrared use the closest available PCB colors.
Confirm exact LED substitutions and fabrication colors before production.
